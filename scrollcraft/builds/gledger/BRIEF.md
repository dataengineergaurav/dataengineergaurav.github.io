### 5. One thing no site they have seen does (signature move seed)
A live provenance ledger / trace rail. As you scroll, a thin fixed trace at the bottom edge accumulates a marker per beat — sector, scale, outcome — so by the footer the trace is a complete record of the journey, doubling as navigation. Passing a case-study row in the Work chapter also stamps its architecture flow (Source → Ingest → Model → Serve) into an accumulative ledger panel. No other consulting site lets you scrub through someone's delivery history as a system diagram that composes itself.

Alternative considered: the paper itself reacting (mustard rule as reading progress). Trace rail chosen as primary; paper reaction kept as secondary atmosphere.

### 6. How far from premium-minimal
Editorial, staying close to premium-minimal. Current site is already premium-minimal editorial; push editorial harder (more ledger, more rule lines, more typographic hierarchy, more asymmetry in scroll acts) rather than swinging to brutalist or maximalist.

### 7. One unbroken world or distinct scenes
Distinct scenes (chapters/cuts). Content is categorically different per section — a promise, a proof strip, capabilities, case studies, voices, a CTA — each deserves its own visual logic. An unbroken worldflight would force false continuity between "300+ pipelines" and "Benjamin Harvey said..."

### 8. What assets do you already have
Effectively nothing photographic. Existing repo has only logo.png, favicon.png, og.png. No footage, no photography, no product shots, no clips of self. Text assets are real: 6 case studies, 2 long-form posts, 3 testimonials. Build assumes fully CSS/photographic-via-tone world plus real text as anchor.
