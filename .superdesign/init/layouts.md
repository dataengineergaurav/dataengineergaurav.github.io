# Layouts

## `_layouts/default.html`

Shared document shell: sticky site header, brand and primary navigation, centered `main.container`, and compact social footer. The Insights page uses this layout.

## `_layouts/post.html`

Article shell with dated eyebrow, title, optional summary, narrow prose body, and consulting CTA.

## `_layouts/project.html`

Case-study shell used by the Work collection. It is not changed by the Insights archive plan.
